ABOUT THE LAB.

Vice City Launch Lab is an independently created fan website built around the excitement for Grand Theft Auto VI. We make lighthearted, shareable interactive tools as well as practical launch-day planning resources.

Editorial approach

We distinguish verified official information from creative fan entertainment and editable cost estimates. We link to Rockstar and platform sources where relevant, and we do not publish supposed leaks as verified news.

Independence

This site is not endorsed by, sponsored by, or affiliated with Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, Sony, or Microsoft. Grand Theft Auto and GTA are marks of their respective owners. This site uses its own illustration and layout rather than copied promotional images.
